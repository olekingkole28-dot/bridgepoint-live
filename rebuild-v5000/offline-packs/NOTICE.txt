GLOBAL BASEMAP
The global vector basemap is extracted from a dated Protomaps Basemap daily build derived from OpenStreetMap. OpenStreetMap data is licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL). Attribution is required. The included global archive is generalized to zoom levels 0–6; it is not a street-level or property-level global dataset.
Source: https://docs.protomaps.com/basemaps/downloads

LIMITS
This package does NOT contain global detailed parcel boundaries, universally complete high-resolution building footprints, detailed 3D buildings, global high-resolution LiDAR, universally redistributable aerial/satellite imagery, or live weather/flight/emergency feeds. Such datasets have different coverage, data-quality, freshness and licensing conditions. Cached information is not live information.
