BridgePoint Intelligence — planetary texture attribution

The Mercury, Venus surface, Venus atmosphere, Jupiter, Uranus and Neptune
equirectangular JPEG texture maps are sourced from the Solar System Scope
planetary texture collection:

  https://phet.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

The collection identifies the textures as CC BY 4.0. The source textures are
downloaded during the production build and served locally by BridgePoint;
the browser does not fetch texture files from a third-party host at runtime.
