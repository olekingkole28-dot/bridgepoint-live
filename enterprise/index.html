National Property Estimate Variance Index

BridgePoint is preparing a reproducible, vendor-neutral benchmark of differences between submitted property repair estimates and independently supported scope/cost evidence. It will separate scope variance from price variance and will not label variance as wrongful “underpayment” without support.
