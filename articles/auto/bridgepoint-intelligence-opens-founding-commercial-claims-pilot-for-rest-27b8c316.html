BridgePoint Intelligence opens founding commercial claims pilot for restoration, roofing and public-adjusting firms

The Connecticut-born property-intelligence platform is recruiting a limited reference cohort to measure daily workflow use and verified claim-stage duration alongside existing claims systems.
Published August 22, 2026 · Automatic public research distribution · Source content passed BridgePoint's customer-safety quality gate.

BridgePoint Intelligence has opened a founding commercial claims pilot for a limited reference cohort of commercial restoration, roofing, exterior-remodeling and public-adjusting organizations.

The pilot is designed to measure real workflow use rather than collect testimonials first. Participating organizations can use BridgePoint property intelligence alongside their existing claims and estimating systems while contributing verified historical or current claim timelines where appropriate.

BridgePoint's study framework records milestones such as first notice of loss, inspection, estimates, supplements, carrier responses, disputes, appraisal, settlement and payment. Company-level case-study readiness requires minimum verified sample thresholds and methods review before any public performance claim is approved.

BridgePoint is currently customer-ready in Connecticut while additional U.S. states continue through explicit data and intelligence readiness gates. The platform's vendor-neutral JSON/CSV export is the current ready interoperability path; native Xactimate/XactAnalysis and Cotality Claims Workspace integrations remain partner-activation-required until formally enabled and verified.

Use limitation: BUILDING describes BridgePoint platform coverage/readiness only. It is not evidence that a property was damaged, a claim exists, or an opportunity is verified.
