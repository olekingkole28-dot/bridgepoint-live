What the book is about

A Map I Didn't Have follows Johnson's path through homelessness and instability into building BridgePoint Intelligence. It is a founder memoir about trying to create an asset without the conventional map—capital, stability, a large team or an established technology company already behind the idea.

About the author

Kole Johnson is the founder and CEO of BridgePoint Intelligence, a U.S. property and claims intelligence platform focused on canonical property identity, geospatial matching, evidence provenance and explainable property-level intelligence.
