BridgePoint GIS / municipal embed

The widget is intended for site owners and partners who deliberately add BridgePoint to their public or internal GIS/property pages.

Simple iframe

<iframe
 src="https://bridgepointintelligence.online/distribution/gis-widget/?address=123%20Main%20St"
 width="100%" height="520" loading="lazy">
</iframe>

JavaScript embed

<div class="bridgepoint-gis-widget"
 data-address="123 Main St"
 data-height="520"></div>
<script src="https://bridgepointintelligence.online/distribution/gis-widget/embed.js" async></script>

Coordinates

?lat=41.55&lng=-72.65&z=17

The widget shows BridgePoint's live 3D map and BII publication status. A withheld BII remains withheld; the widget does not fabricate a score.
