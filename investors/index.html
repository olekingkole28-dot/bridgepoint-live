Why this exists

Property decisions are still fragmented across government records, weather systems, field evidence, claims documents, imagery, risk models and workflow tools. BridgePoint's thesis is that these pieces become more useful when they resolve back to one property identity and one explainable timeline.

What compounds

The core system is designed around reusable source onboarding, normalization, property matching, provenance, confidence, scoring, opportunity logic, entitlement controls and customer outcomes. New verticals can reuse the same property intelligence spine rather than rebuild identity and evidence plumbing from scratch.
