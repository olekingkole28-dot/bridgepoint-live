WEATHER INTELLIGENCE · SEPTEMBER 26, 2026

Nor'easter brings damaging wind, coastal flooding and heavy rain to the Northeast.

BridgePoint is tracking the storm through official National Weather Service feeds and its live weather layers. This page is informational; NWS alerts and local emergency instructions remain authoritative.

What official forecasters are reporting

The National Weather Service Boston/Norton forecast discussion describes a strong, long-duration Nor'easter with damaging wind gusts, coastal flooding and beach erosion, periods of heavy rain, and power-outage concerns — especially across Connecticut, Rhode Island and eastern Massachusetts.

Coastal and southeast ConnecticutNWS New York has posted wind-advisory conditions including north winds around 25–35 mph with gusts up to 55 mph in affected areas.
Southern Middlesex and Southern New London CountiesNWS coastal-flood guidance calls for roughly 1–2 feet of inundation above ground level in vulnerable shoreline locations during the advisory period.

Why BridgePoint tracks this spatially

A storm is not one number. Wind, flood, rain, roads, structures, roofs and parcel boundaries overlap differently block by block. BridgePoint's live globe is designed to put those hazards beside property and building context so users can see where exposure and operational attention may concentrate.
