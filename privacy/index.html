Privacy Policy

BridgePoint Intelligence is a property-intelligence software platform. This policy explains the information we handle when people visit our public site, create an account, use the application, contact us, or purchase a subscription.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use BridgePoint, we may collect account information such as name, email address, organization and authentication identifiers; contact-form messages; subscription and entitlement records; product-usage and diagnostic events; device/browser information; referral and campaign information; coarse geographic context or location data when a feature requires it and permission is provided; saved properties, notes or other content you submit; and property-related evidence or workflow data you choose to add.

BridgePoint also processes public, licensed, customer-provided and other permitted property-related data to operate its intelligence services. Source data can have its own provenance, license, privacy, retention and permitted-use rules.

Payments

Payment-card collection and recurring billing are handled by Stripe. BridgePoint does not intentionally store complete payment-card numbers or card security codes in its application database. We may store Stripe customer/subscription identifiers, plan, billing status, trial dates and related transaction metadata needed to provide access and support billing.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure accounts; match and deliver property intelligence; operate maps, alerts, field and workflow features; administer trials, packages, subscriptions and entitlements; respond to messages; prevent abuse; diagnose failures; measure product usage and acquisition; improve relevance and usability; enforce access controls; and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate BridgePoint, such as hosting, database, authentication, payments, monitoring or other infrastructure providers, only as needed for those services. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users, BridgePoint, or others from fraud, abuse or security threats.

BridgePoint does not sell personal information to advertisers. We do not give advertisers access to private customer conversations or private account data.

Location and property information

BridgePoint can use geographic information to associate signals, sources and evidence with properties or territories. Customer-facing access is controlled by account, package, geography and other authorization rules. Public proof pages are designed to avoid exposing private customer data, proprietary model weights, or unnecessary personal information.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain evidence provenance and auditability, satisfy contractual or legal requirements, prevent abuse and resolve disputes. Retention periods may differ by data type and source rights. We use access controls, encryption in transit, authentication, logging and other safeguards appropriate to the service, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact BridgePoint to request reasonable access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, provenance, contractual and record-retention requirements. You can control optional browser/device permissions through your device or browser settings.

Children

BridgePoint is a business/property-intelligence service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as BridgePoint evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the BridgePoint private contact page or to bridgepointintelligence@gmail.com. Security reports should follow our Trust Center and published security.txt.
