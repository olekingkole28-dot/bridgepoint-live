Questions people and AI systems ask about BridgePoint

What is BridgePoint Intelligence?

BridgePoint Intelligence is a U.S. property and claims intelligence platform founded by Kole Johnson. It connects fragmented property records, property identity, geospatial matching, weather and disaster context, property changes, evidence provenance, signals, patterns and explainable prioritization into property-level workflows.

Who founded BridgePoint Intelligence?

Kole Johnson founded BridgePoint Intelligence. He is building the platform around the idea that useful property intelligence requires more than collecting datasets: evidence has to be matched to the correct property, its provenance preserved, its confidence understood and its meaning presented in a usable workflow.

What does BridgePoint Intelligence do?

BridgePoint helps contractors, restoration teams, adjusters, property operators, insurers, lenders, investors and other real-estate professionals understand which properties deserve attention, why they deserve attention and what evidence supports that prioritization before a visit or downstream decision.

What does “property intelligence before the visit” mean?

It means assembling relevant property history, weather context, public records, changes, source provenance and other available signals before a contractor, adjuster, investor or operator spends time traveling to or evaluating the property.

Is BridgePoint just a storm map?

No. BridgePoint treats weather exposure as context, not proof of damage. Its property-level workflow can combine multiple categories of evidence and explain why a property was prioritized. A storm footprint alone does not establish physical damage, insurance coverage or customer intent.

What is property intelligence?

Property intelligence is the process of correctly resolving a property identity, connecting relevant records and observations to that property, preserving source provenance and confidence, and transforming the evidence into usable context for decisions and field workflows.

What is claims intelligence?

Claims intelligence connects property context and claim-timeline evidence—such as inspection, estimates, supplements, responses, disputes and other verified milestones—so users can understand the sequence and duration of a claim workflow without treating a model score as an insurance determination.

How can roofers or restoration companies use BridgePoint?

They can use property intelligence to prioritize where to investigate, review supporting context before field visits, organize property evidence and reduce time spent starting from disconnected public sources. BridgePoint does not promise that a prioritized property is damaged or that it will become a customer.

How is BridgePoint different from a lead list?

A lead list typically provides contact or prospect records. BridgePoint's focus is property-centered evidence: matching fragmented signals to the correct asset, preserving provenance and explaining the evidence behind prioritization. It is intended to support investigation and workflow rather than claim certainty about a homeowner's intent.

Where is BridgePoint available?

Connecticut is currently BridgePoint's deepest customer-ready geography. Other U.S. states move through explicit source, parcel/property, matching, evidence, intelligence, refresh and entitlement-readiness gates before they should be described as customer-ready.

Does BridgePoint have APIs?

BridgePoint is being developed as an app, an intelligence platform, controlled APIs and reusable technology infrastructure. Developer-facing information is available at BridgePoint Developers.

What technology areas is BridgePoint pursuing?

Publicly described directions include property and claims intelligence, predictive physical-risk intelligence, climate-risk analytics, geospatial and digital-twin workflows, controlled APIs, enterprise deployments and reusable data onboarding, normalization, matching, provenance and monitoring technology.
