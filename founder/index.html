BridgePoint Intelligence · Founder

Kole Johnson

Founder of BridgePoint Intelligence, a U.S. property and claims intelligence platform built to connect fragmented property evidence into explainable operational intelligence.

What he is building

Johnson founded BridgePoint Intelligence to help contractors, restoration teams, adjusters, property operators, insurers, lenders, investors and other property professionals understand a property before a visit or downstream decision. BridgePoint combines property identity resolution, public/property records, geospatial matching, weather and disaster context, property changes, provenance, evidence, signals, patterns and explainable prioritization.

What BridgePoint is trying to solve

Property information is fragmented across government systems, public records, weather sources, parcel data, building records and other datasets. BridgePoint's core thesis is that the hard problem is not merely collecting more data; it is correctly matching evidence to the right property, preserving provenance, measuring confidence and turning it into a usable workflow.

Current national build

BridgePoint currently reports 169,664,739 canonical U.S. property/parcel identity records, alongside 146,586,444 normalized/public address records, 143,709,936 map-ready buildings, and separately measured parcel-boundary and property↔boundary layers. The public machine-readable facts endpoint reports the current definitions and freshness.

National opportunity estimate

Using BridgePoint's current strict Connecticut verified-opportunity yield as a transparent calibration benchmark, the platform's current completion estimate is approximately 95,562 strict verified property opportunities across all 56 supported U.S. states and jurisdictions after the present national intelligence backlog is processed. This is a modeled estimate, not a claim that 95,562 opportunities are already verified today. Read the methodology and jurisdiction breakdown.
