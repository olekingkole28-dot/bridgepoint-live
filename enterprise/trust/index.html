Data governance

Purpose, provenance and access controls

BridgePoint distinguishes rights to use data in a customer workflow from rights to publish it or reuse it. Property, location, claim, sensor and other evidence can have separate privacy, license, verification, retention and purpose limits. Protected customer data and proprietary scoring logic are not published through public proof pages.
