# BridgePoint workflow-native distribution v5600

This release adds ready-to-submit/source packages for:
- Google Sheets;
- QGIS;
- Postman;
- MCP-compatible AI clients;
- Databricks Marketplace.

It builds on distribution infrastructure that already existed before v5600:
- BII browser extension;
- GIS/municipal iframe widget;
- public exposure feeds;
- OpenAPI contract;
- partner delivery contract;
- deep links;
- public evidence audit.

The goal is not another marketing page. It is to make BridgePoint usable from software where property, GIS and data teams already work.
