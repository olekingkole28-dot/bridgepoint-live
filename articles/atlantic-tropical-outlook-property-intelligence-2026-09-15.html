ATLANTIC TROPICAL OUTLOOK · SEPTEMBER 15, 2026

Atlantic disturbance: 40% seven-day development chance.

The National Hurricane Center reported no active Atlantic tropical cyclone in its September 15 early-morning outlook, but identified a central subtropical Atlantic trough that could become a tropical depression later in the week.

~0%NHC formation chance through 48 hours
40%NHC formation chance through 7 days

NHC described disorganized showers and thunderstorms associated with a trough well east-southeast of Bermuda. Environmental conditions were expected to become more conducive for development in a few days, with a tropical depression possible late in the week as the system moved slowly west-northwestward.
