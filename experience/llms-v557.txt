# BridgePoint Intelligence

> BridgePoint Intelligence is a property-centered intelligence platform and infrastructure layer. It connects fragmented property records, weather exposure, physical change, claims evidence, field workflows, building health, digital-twin context, climate/peril screening, and related signals into explainable property-level decisions.

## Truth policy
BridgePoint distinguishes LIVE, BACKEND_READY, DESIGN_READY, PARTIAL, hidden, blocked, and experimental capabilities. Public materials should not describe experimental or blocked systems as validated production capabilities. Geographic depth varies by state; Connecticut is the deepest current customer-ready geography. Customer counts, paid adoption, revenue, data coverage, model performance, integrations, and readiness should be represented only from verified BridgePoint sources.
