The operating battle

Static history is necessary. Live state is the expansion layer.

Archive-oriented property intelligence

Past records, imagery, claims history and snapshots remain valuable context. BridgePoint consumes those inputs where lawful and useful.

BridgePoint live-state architecture

The roadmap adds active weather monitoring, authorized IoT/BAS observations, field evidence, material events and versioned digital-twin updates so the property record can change as the asset changes.

Current truth: BridgePoint has an active five-minute pre-storm weather worker and implemented IoT/twin data models. Customer IoT telemetry still requires authorized building or sensor connections; BridgePoint does not imply live sensor coverage where none exists.

Human-review gates

Near-term monetization wedge

Smart Supplement Detection

BridgePoint’s implemented claims workflow normalizes evidence-backed repair scope, compares it with parsed estimate line items, flags omissions, quantity differences and price variance, and can assemble a human-review supplement-support packet.

Scope variance

Identify supported repair items or quantities that differ from the submitted estimate.

Price variance

Compare authorized/local pricing evidence when lawful pricing inputs are available.

Documentation packet

Organize evidence and the comparison for a contractor, adjuster or authorized professional to review before submission.

Current truth: the backend capability exists but is still in SHADOW validation and has no real estimate runs recorded yet. BridgePoint does not promise a supplement will compel payment and does not convert a cost difference into a legal conclusion.

The startup constraint

Contribution with rights

Real users can contribute claim timelines, field evidence and outcomes only under explicit rights, de-identification and verification rules.

Interchange instead of lock-in dependence

Canonical claim schemas and export mappings are designed to let BridgePoint ingest or exchange permitted data without assuming privileged access to a vendor’s proprietary systems.

Workflow → labeled evidence

Every verified inspection, estimate comparison, repair outcome or sensor-confirmed condition can become a future validation candidate when data rights allow.

Enterprise architecture

Privacy and rights gates

Quiet-weather retention

Evidence flywheel

Digital-twin progression

A partial twin can improve as BIM, LiDAR, imagery, drone data, blueprints and sensors arrive; BridgePoint does not call a low-fidelity twin an exact replica.

Deep-tech discipline

Experimental sensing stays visibly validation-stage until modality, calibration and professional-confirmation gates are satisfied.

Open technical proof

National Property Estimate Variance Index

BridgePoint is preparing a reproducible, vendor-neutral benchmark of differences between submitted property repair estimates and independently supported scope/cost evidence. It separates scope variance from price variance and will not label variance as wrongful “underpayment” without support.

Open registry + methodology

Current status: data collection. No vendor-specific conclusion is published until sample-size, de-identification, data-rights and methodology-review gates pass.
