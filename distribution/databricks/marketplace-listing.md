# BridgePoint U.S. Property + Building Intelligence

BridgePoint Intelligence connects canonical property identity, parcel context, building geometry and source lineage in a spatially resolved U.S. property layer.

## Designed for
- insurance and catastrophe analytics
- commercial real estate and site selection
- proptech enrichment
- geospatial analytics
- property-data QA
- AI/agent workflows that require grounded property identity

## What the sample demonstrates
Use an address to resolve a BridgePoint property and inspect public-safe identifiers, coordinates, parcel references, building detail and source context.

## Data governance
BridgePoint distinguishes source-backed facts, derived attributes and unavailable evidence. Public distribution never exposes privileged service credentials or silently upgrades account-gated intelligence into public data.
