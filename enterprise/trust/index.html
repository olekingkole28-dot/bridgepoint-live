Data governance

Operational use is not the same as publication or model reuse.

BridgePoint distinguishes rights to use data in a customer workflow from rights to publish it or reuse it as model-training/validation evidence. Claim, location, sensor and property evidence can be subject to separate privacy, license, verification, retention and purpose limits.
