What BridgePoint does

Not another raw lead list.

BridgePoint is built around the question that matters before a drive, canvass, inspection or acquisition review: which properties deserve human attention first, and why?

1 · Match

Unify fragmented property evidence

Normalize addresses, parcels and public or permitted sources so evidence is tied to the right property instead of floating in disconnected datasets.

2 · Explain

Turn records into signals

Preserve dates, provenance and evidence families so users can see why a property ranked—not just receive an opaque score.

3 · Prioritize

Spend field time where it matters

Use the intelligence layer to narrow a territory and prepare before the first site visit. Field verification still has the last word.
