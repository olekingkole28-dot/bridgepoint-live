Why BridgePoint publishes this

Most property-data marketing stops at claims such as “AI-powered,” “storm intelligence” or “better leads.” BridgePoint's research program is intended to expose measurable aggregate outputs and methodology limitations. The core thesis is that useful property intelligence depends on correctly resolving evidence to the right property, preserving provenance and confidence, and explaining why the evidence deserves attention.

Methodology limitation: a BridgePoint verified opportunity is a prioritization candidate, not proof of physical damage, insurance coverage, customer intent, a claim outcome or a guaranteed sale. Weather exposure is context, not proof of damage. Field verification remains necessary.
