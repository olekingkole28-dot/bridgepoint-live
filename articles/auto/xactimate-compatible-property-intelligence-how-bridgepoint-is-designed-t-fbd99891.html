Xactimate-compatible property intelligence: how BridgePoint is designed to fit existing claims workflows

BridgePoint is building vendor-neutral claim and property exports now while keeping native Xactimate activation truthfully gated behind authorized integration.
Published August 22, 2026 · Automatic public research distribution · Source content passed BridgePoint's customer-safety quality gate.

Claims professionals should not have to abandon the systems they already use just to benefit from a new intelligence layer. BridgePoint is therefore being structured around a vendor-neutral claim and property record that can move into existing workflows.

The current BridgePoint export contract can package verified property identity, evidence references, claim-timeline events and derived stage durations into clean JSON or CSV. That is usable today as a portable interchange format.

Native write-back into Verisk Xactimate or XactAnalysis is a different standard. BridgePoint does not label that integration live until the required partner or customer activation exists and production behavior is verified. The same rule applies to Cotality Claims Workspace and Symbility-lineage workflows.

This distinction matters because interoperability is part of product trust. A credible integration strategy should make data portable immediately, state precisely which native connectors require authorization and preserve enough structure that an approved adapter can be added without rebuilding the underlying intelligence model.

Use limitation: BridgePoint public research is decision-support. It does not prove property damage, claim coverage, customer intent, a permit, or a guaranteed outcome.
