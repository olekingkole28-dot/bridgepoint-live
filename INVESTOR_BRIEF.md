# BridgePoint Intelligence — Investor Brief

BridgePoint Intelligence is building **property-centered intelligence infrastructure** for property, insurance, claims, climate-risk, construction and field workflows.

**Investor page:** https://bridgepointintelligence.online/investors/

## Thesis

Property decisions are still fragmented across parcel and assessment records, permits, weather and disaster systems, physical-property changes, imagery, claims evidence, field observations and workflow software. BridgePoint's core thesis is that these sources become substantially more useful when they resolve back to a durable property identity, an explainable evidence timeline and controlled decision workflows.

## Architecture

**BridgePoint App → BridgePoint Intelligence Platform → BridgePoint APIs → BridgePoint Technology Infrastructure**

The application is the operating wedge. The compounding infrastructure is the reusable machinery for source onboarding, normalization, deduplication, geospatial alignment, property matching, provenance, confidence, scoring, opportunity logic, entitlement control, monitoring and refresh.

## Strategic directions

- Property and claims intelligence for contractors, adjusters and operators
- Carrier and enterprise risk-intelligence products
- Climate and physical-risk analytics
- Portfolio-level property intelligence
- Controlled APIs and data products
- Digital-twin and building-health workflows
- Private/enterprise deployments and custom geography onboarding

## Commercial posture

BridgePoint distinguishes between live, customer-eligible, screening, validation and integration-required capabilities. Public materials are designed not to convert weather exposure or model signals into unsupported claims of physical damage, insurance coverage or guaranteed outcomes.

Connecticut is the deepest customer-ready geography. Additional U.S. markets move through explicit property, source, matching, evidence, refresh and entitlement-readiness gates.
