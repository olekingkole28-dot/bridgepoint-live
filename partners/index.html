Founding partner rules

  • Commission eligibility requires manual BridgePoint approval; receiving a tracking link is not approval.
  • Approved founding rate: 30% of settled net subscription revenue for the first six paid billing months of an eligible new customer.
  • The referral tracking window is 60 days. BridgePoint records a valid referral code across the customer journey.
  • Self-referrals, internal/test accounts, fraudulent traffic, refunded payments and chargebacks are excluded.
  • Commission is calculated on net subscription revenue actually received by BridgePoint after refunds, chargebacks and applicable transaction reversals.
  • Payout method and payment details are confirmed with approved partners before any payout is due.
  • Partners must describe BridgePoint accurately. Property intelligence and weather context are decision-support signals, not proof of physical damage, insurance coverage, claim outcome or guaranteed sales.

Questions can be handled through BridgePoint's normal contact route. This page does not enroll anyone into an automatic payment contract.
