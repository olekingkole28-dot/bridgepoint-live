About BridgePoint Intelligence

BridgePoint Intelligence is a Connecticut-based software platform built to turn fragmented property, environmental and permitted public/licensed evidence into explainable property intelligence and practical workflows.

What BridgePoint does

BridgePoint connects property identity, source provenance, environmental context, physical-change information, evidence, signals, scoring and workflow tools so authorized users can understand why a property deserves attention and what evidence supports that view.

Who it is for

The platform is designed for property-focused professionals and organizations such as contractors, restoration teams, adjusters, property managers, real-estate operators and enterprise users who need better property context before acting.

How we handle trust

BridgePoint separates public proof from protected customer data and proprietary scoring logic. Customer access is controlled by authentication, package, geography and role. Public capability labels are intended to distinguish live, screening, validation and building-stage functionality.
