The lifecycle model

01 · PRE-LOSS

Risk & baseline

Property identity, assessments, roof/building context, hazard outlooks, environmental context and historical events establish the pre-event baseline.

02 · LOSS

Event & evidence

Warnings, observed weather, wildfire, flood, earthquake, fire incidents and other source-labelled events establish event context—not automatic causation.

03 · CLAIM

Claim-stage context

Public/authorized claim and policy context, stage timelines, estimates, supplements, disputes and regulatory recovery belong here when the source and rights permit it.

04 · RECOVERY

Assistance & recovery

Disaster declarations, individual/public assistance, mitigation and recovery programs connect the loss event to recovery infrastructure.

05 · REPAIR / REBUILD

Work & closeout

Permits, contractor licensing, inspections and occupancy/closeout can show repair activity when an authoritative source is available and legally usable.

06 · PROPERTY / FINANCIAL

Ongoing property state

Assessment, sales/deeds/liens and financial-distress context are separate evidence families with their own privacy and use constraints.

Public operating rules

Source provenance survives

Provider, source URL or identifier, observation time and uncertainty should remain attached to downstream analytics.

Rights before ingestion

Restricted, licensed, paid, private or high-privacy sources remain blocked or context-only until the relevant rights/privacy review passes.

No fake urgency

BridgePoint does not disguise marketing as a critical error, bug bounty, emergency ticket or mandatory system notification.

Opt-in delivery

Feeds can be consumed by people and systems that choose to subscribe. Direct partner/API delivery requires an authorized integration.
