# BridgePoint Roof Squares & Waste Calculator

A tiny local-first browser calculator for converting measured roof area into roofing squares and estimating waste-adjusted material area.

- No login
- No uploads
- No analytics or tracking
- Runs entirely in the browser
- MIT licensed

Live tool: https://bridgepointintelligence.online/free-tools/roof-squares/

## Formula

- Base squares = roof area in square feet / 100
- Adjusted area = roof area × (1 + selected waste factor)
- Adjusted squares = adjusted area / 100

This is a material-planning calculator, not a roof measurement, code, engineering, or ordering recommendation. Verify field measurements, roof complexity, manufacturer instructions, and applicable requirements before ordering material.
