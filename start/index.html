1. Start with a property.

BridgePoint resolves the property identity, geography and available evidence so weather, public records, timelines and customer work are tied to the same asset instead of scattered across tabs.

Example: one property record → parcel context → relevant events → evidence timeline.
⛈️

2. Add weather and peril context.

BridgePoint brings weather/peril context into the property record and can prioritize properties affected by meaningful events. Alerts and modeled exposure remain evidence context—not automatic proof of damage.

Free preview: try the NWS Claim Weather Formatter.
🔎

3. Understand “why this property?”

Signals, patterns, provenance and confidence are combined into explainable opportunity context. The goal is to show the evidence behind prioritization rather than hand users an unexplained score.

BridgePoint question: What happened? Why does it matter now? What evidence supports the conclusion?
📋

4. Claims can go deeper.

Smart Supplement Detection is designed to compare authorized, normalized estimate lines against evidence-supported repair scope and flag missing items, quantity shortfalls and price variance for human review.

Important: a variance is not automatically coverage owed, legal underpayment or guaranteed carrier payment.
⚡

5. Use BridgePoint without waiting on us.

Create an account, answer the short workspace setup, and enter the intelligence environment. BridgePoint is being built so a customer can discover, learn and use the product without booking a founder demo.
