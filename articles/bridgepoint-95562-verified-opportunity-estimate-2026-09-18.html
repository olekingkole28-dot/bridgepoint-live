What “verified opportunity” means

A BridgePoint VERIFIED opportunity means the record has passed the platform's current evidence and quality gates as a candidate deserving human review. It does not automatically prove physical damage, insurance coverage, customer intent, a permit, a claim outcome, or a guaranteed sale.
