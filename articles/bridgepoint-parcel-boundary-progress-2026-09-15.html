Search results often flatten property-data scale into a single “parcel count.” BridgePoint intentionally does not. One property identity may exist before boundary geometry is materialized; a boundary can exist before a high-confidence property link is complete; multi-unit and vertical property identities can further complicate simple one-row-equals-one-parcel assumptions.
